# Structural Trend Retest V2

This candidate replaces the idea that any moving-average touch is a retest.
It requires a pre-existing uptrend, a close through measured resistance on
expanded volume, a bounded multi-bar pullback on lower volume, continued closes
near or above the broken level, and a bullish rejection back above it.

The stop is below the observed retest structure with an ATR buffer. Parameters
are frozen before evaluation. The strategy remains research-only and must pass
cost, breadth, chronological, tail-risk, untouched holdout, and prospective
Paper gates before any allocation is considered.
